- [ ] **Real local weather in the player's own town (optional).**
  - Now: the animated town, fields and map ([docs/features/town-animations.md](docs/features/town-animations.md), Rules and numbers, Day, night and weather) draw cosmetic weather chosen with seeded randomness for each game state and four-hour block, so everyone in a state sees the same sky; night follows the player's clock at fixed hours (full from 21:00 to 05:00). Acts of god aren't built yet ([docs/features/market-and-factions.md](docs/features/market-and-factions.md), Acts of god).
  - Plan: let a player see their real local weather over their own Town and Fields views, without anything invasive:
    - **Rough location from the time zone only:** the browser's `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone` (e.g. `Europe/Dublin`) needs no permission and is already visible to every website. The time zone database (`zone.tab`) gives each zone a representative city's coordinates: roughly country-level, which is all a sky needs. Never the browser's geolocation API (a permission prompt, precise coordinates), and no IP lookup (invisible to the player, personal data under GDPR, wrong behind a VPN).
    - **Weather fetched by our server, not the browser:** so no third party sees the player's IP. The server looks up each time zone's weather every 30-60 minutes and caches it (a few hundred zones at most), and the client asks our API for its zone's (a new endpoint in `shared/contracts`, then `npm run gen:api`). Nothing is stored against the player, and the zone isn't logged per player.
    - **A weather service:** Open-Meteo (free, no key; the free tier is non-commercial, attribution asked) or MET Norway (free, CC BY 4.0, needs an identifying user-agent and attribution). Check the terms before choosing. Their WMO weather codes map onto ours: drizzle and rain to rain, snow to snow, thunder to storm; fog and overcast could become new skies.
    - **Real sunrise and sunset**, worked out in the browser from the time zone's coordinates and the date, with no network at all: nights follow the season and place instead of fixed hours. This could ship on its own first.
    - **A setting in Player settings**, with a plain note (e.g. "Uses your device's time zone to show your local weather. Your location is never asked for or stored."), worded in every locale.
    - **Personal, not the world's:** the map, other players' views and any game event keep the game's weather; when acts of god exist, a game storm overrides the real sky, since it's part of the game. If anything fails (no zone, the service down), the town falls back to the game's weather.
  - To decide: on or off by default; which weather service, and its terms and credit; how often to refresh; whether to add fog and overcast skies; whether sunrise and sunset ship first.

## Done

Newest first, with the date it was finished.

- 2026-10-23: **Better hero names.** Heroes are named for the state they come from, in its culture (Frisian, Saxon, Franconian, Lotharingian, Czech, Carinthian, Italian): a first name with a real place of the state, a father's name, or both, in the local form ("Gero von Meissen", "Ugo di Guido", "Jitka Borivojova"), in plain letters and at most 20 characters, never repeating the player's other heroes while it can. Half are women (a new `heroes.sex` column, migration 0029, not shown); about 1 Inn hero in 5 is a traveller from another state. Inn offers, hero boxes, the admin's grant and NPC guards use it; heroes waiting in an Inn are renamed at worker start-up, keeping their stats. See [docs/features/hero-names.md](docs/features/hero-names.md).
- 2026-10-22: **`no-unnecessary-condition` on.** Casts that hid a miss became honest lookups (`fortKeyOf`, `troopKey`), dead fallbacks on always-present data went, and four lines the rule can't follow carry a disable with the reason (docs/features/unchecked-index-access.md; AGENTS.md, Lookups can miss).
- 2026-10-22: **No `!` in source code.** The 87 non-null assertions outside tests are gone (guards return what they checked: `requireRank`, `assertCanUpgrade`, `assertCanTrain`, `claimsOf(req)`, `scheduledWith`), and `no-non-null-assertion` is on everywhere but tests (AGENTS.md, Lookups can miss).
- 2026-10-22: **Lookups that can miss are typed as such.** `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` is on in both tsconfigs, after fixing about 1,540 errors in chunks kept from slipping back by a check: `one(rows)` for rows that must be there, the battle simulator on literal index types, `distributeLossesAmong`, `pairs()` and `moved()` in the client, tuples for fixed lists, and `!` in tests only (docs/features/unchecked-index-access.md; AGENTS.md, Lookups can miss).
- 2026-10-21: **A proof-of-work puzzle on sign-up.** The sign-up form fetches a signed puzzle (`GET /auth/challenge`, ALTCHA's `altcha-lib`, without its widget) and solves it in a worker while the player types: PBKDF2/SHA-256, cost 1,000, a hidden counter of 5,000-10,000 (about 0.5 s on a desktop, measured; a few seconds on a slow phone, estimated). `POST /auth/register` checks it and accepts each puzzle once, again only if the name was taken; puzzles expire after 10 minutes and the form renews them before then. Signed with a secret derived from `JWT_SECRET`; settings `SIGNUP_POW_*` in docs/deployment.md. It makes accounts made in bulk by a script cost something, not impossible (a GPU computes PBKDF2 far faster).
- 2026-10-21: **Wrong passwords wait.** After 5 wrong passwords in a row for a username, each further login waits 1 s, 2 s, 4 s … up to 5 minutes (429 `auth.tooManyTries`, with Retry-After), even with the right password; the right password or 15 minutes without a wrong one starts it again. Counted per username, never per IP, and alike for names with no account; kept in memory (`backend/services/api/src/auth/login-backoff.ts`, settings `LOGIN_*` in docs/deployment.md).
- 2026-10-21: **Workspace dependencies checked with sherif**, part of `npm run lint` (AGENTS.md, Commands).
- 2026-10-21: **New dependencies pinned by default:** `.npmrc` sets `save-exact=true`, so `npm i` writes exact versions.
- 2026-10-21: **Lint with types, and with the project's conventions.** typescript-eslint `recommendedTypeChecked`, regexp, jsx-a11y-x, TanStack Query, Drizzle and vitest rules, the client's conventions (no `confirm`, `fetch` or `toLocaleString`) and a locale test against "lord"; `noImplicitReturns` and `noFallthroughCasesInSwitch` everywhere. Fixed what they found: unhandled promises from async handlers, `[object File]` from form fields, untyped army bodies sent to the API, the WebSocket's missing types (`@types/ws`) and React's deprecated `FormEvent` (AGENTS.md, Formatting and linting).
