# Colonies and suzerains in Evony Age 2

**Version:** Evony Age 2. Age 1 had no colonies. Recorded as a model for what a historic city's holder might do with the cities round it (features/market-and-factions.md, Historical cities: tribute, drafting).

**Sources** (read 2026-10-19; facts in our own words):

2. *Suzerain*, Evony Wiki: <https://evony.fandom.com/wiki/Suzerain>

4. *Draft*, Evony Wiki: <https://evony.fandom.com/wiki/Draft>

## Taking a colony

A player could colonise another player's city, becoming its **suzerain**:

1. **Declare war** on the player. That took 30 minutes, with a 5-minute wait before declaring on someone else. An item skipped the 30 minutes.
2. **Send an attack with the Colonize mission.** If it won, an 8-hour suppression period began, during which both sides could send reinforcements.
3. **The suppression battle** at the end of the 8 hours fought everything both sides had sent. Win that too, and the city became a colony.

## What the suzerain got

- **Tribute:** a share of the colony's production of all five resources, paid automatically as extra production in the suzerain's city.
- **One policy a day,** chosen from four:
  - **Draft:** take a share of the colony's troops at once.
  - **Disarm:** destroy a share of the colony's troops, without getting them.
  - **Population redeploy:** take a share of the colony's idle population.
  - **Sow discord:** lower the loyalty of one of the colony's heroes. Repeated over several days, the hero could be won and then persuaded with medals.
- **Vague intelligence:** the suzerain saw the colony's troops as words rather than numbers (below).
- **Many colonies:** a fully promoted player could hold something like 400 colonies per city (source 2), which made colonising the usual way to grow.
- Colonies of players inactive for a while could no longer be drafted, so farming inactive players stopped working (source 4).

## Holding one

- **Suppression setting:** the share of each troop type in the suzerain's city that would fight if the colony rose up, 100% by default.
  - Set high, it invited a "backdoor": enemies attacked the colony to make the suzerain's own troops fight there, reaching an army whose gates were closed.
  - Players often set it low, or to 0 for an inactive colony.
- **Abandoning:** handing a colony over took 24 hours (an item made it instant), freeing the slot for a better colony, or for a weaker ally.

## Getting free

The colonised player could rise up. Eight hours later came the uprising battle against the suzerain's suppression army. Winning it freed the city and ended the tribute.

## Troop counts as words

How a suzerain saw a colony's troops (source 3):

| Word | Troops | Word | Troops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Few | 1–24 | Swarm | 500–999 |
| Pack | 25–49 | Zounds | 1,000–2,499 |
| Lots | 50–99 | Legion | 2,500–4,999 |
| Horde | 100–249 | Bulk | 5,000–10,000 |
| Throng | 250–499 | Giga | over 10,000 |

## Ideas for Marchlands

The holder of a seat (a historic city) over NPC cities is close to a suzerain over colonies:
- tribute from the area's production or trade;
- one policy a day (draft, take population, perhaps disarm a rival's NPC allies);
- unrest in place of an uprising.

Drafting other *players* would be a much bigger step. Our deviations already took away capturing players' cities (lords' cities have no loyalty, deviations.md), and colonising them would bring back much of what that removed.
