# Level 12 historic city names

The level 12 historic cities had names in Age 2, like the higher levels. The only list found (searched 2026-11-03) covers seven of the sixteen states, from a player's post on the official forum; a few more names are known for the other nine. Facts only (names and coordinates), sorted by place; see [historic-cities.md](historic-cities.md), Names.

**Version:** Age 2.

**Source:** *Complete list of Historic Cities*, tindris, Evony Age II forums, 6 December 2010, thread 160140 (marked "work in progress"; the forum is gone, read from the Internet Archive's copy of 13 March 2016): <https://web.archive.org/web/20160313081727/http://bbs.evony.com/showthread.php?160140-complete-list-of-historic-cities>. The Evony Wiki's state pages (for example <https://evony.fandom.com/wiki/Swabia>) list each state's level 14s and 16 and point to this thread for the 121 level 12s.

- **Covered:** Friesland, Saxony, North March, Lower Lorraine, Bohemia, Burgundy and Franconia (its level 14 and 16 names match ours). Later replies promised Thuringia and a combined map; neither is in the archived thread.
- **Not covered:** Upper Lorraine, Swabia, Thuringia, Moravia, Lombardy, Tuscany, Bavaria, Carinthia and Romagna. Seven of their level 12s are named elsewhere:
  - the Evony Wiki's *Historic City* page (<https://evony.fandom.com/wiki/Historic_City>): St. Gallen (369, 657) in Lombardy and Vigevano (567, 747) in Tuscany;
  - an Age 2 server emulator's tests (below), checked on a live server in October 2026: Jihlava (243, 585), Chomutov (261, 585) and Prerov (279, 585) in Swabia, Townsville (675, 279) in Moravia and Hong Kong (783, 783) in Romagna; and San Jose (9, 9), as in the list.
- **Where the full list is** (searched 2026-11-03): in the original Flash client, `EvonyClient1921.swf`, as its `Lang2` table: every historic city's name, in order, with the description the client shows. *EvIIEmulator* (<https://github.com/Leitheon/EvIIEmulator>, `src/gamedata.js` and `src/game/historic.js`), a server for that client that ships none of its data, reads the table from the player's own copy and places the names: Atlantis, then the 16 capitals row by row, then the level 14 grid row by row (a name that comes again later is kept for a level 12), then the level 12 grid row by row from (9, 9) to (783, 783), San Jose first and Hong Kong last. No copy of the table itself was found online.
- **How many there were:** the emulator, from a live server in October 2026, leaves out any level 12 within 9 tiles of a bigger historic city (none at (99, 99) beside Washington, D.C., or at (621, 297) beside Harare): 1,795 level 12s, where the grid has 1,936 places. Marchlands follows it since migration 0042 (2026-11-03): with Romagna's two empty level 14 places, which clear nothing, the rule leaves exactly 1,795. The 2010 list names 62 of those places (Olathe at (99, 99), Boston at (99, 27) …), so either the servers changed or the emulator's rule is an approximation.
- **What they're called:** mostly towns and cities of North America (Friesland, Saxony, North March, Bohemia), the Pacific, South America, Turkey and Korea (Lower Lorraine, Franconia) and Europe (Burgundy), with small countries' capitals along the top row of the map (Beirut, Vatican City, Panama City …), where the higher levels are named after national capitals.
- **Checked against the grid** (every 18 tiles from 9): 838 of the 847 places in these states are named once and correctly; the exceptions are below, by state. Spellings are kept as posted (Frederiction, Jacksonvile, Villede Qu?bec …), as the game's own may have differed.
